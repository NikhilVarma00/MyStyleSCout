Puma vs Skechers: Which is Better in India? (2026)

Updated 4 November 2026 · Prices tracked across Amazon, Flipkart & Myntra

When it comes to everyday footwear in India, two brands dominate the conversation: Puma and Skechers. Both are massive globally, but they cater to completely different types of buyers.

Are you looking for a stylish sneaker to wear to the gym and hang out with friends? Or do you want a cloud-like shoe for standing on your feet all day? We compared the data to give you the definitive answer.

The Quick Verdict

Choose Puma if you want sporty style, athletic performance, and frequent heavy discounts on Amazon/Flipkart. Their Smashic and X-Ray models are fantastic value.

Choose Skechers if your #1 priority is walking comfort. Their memory foam and Arch Fit technology is unmatched for long days on your feet.

Comfort & Durability Breakdown

1. Initial Comfort: Skechers Wins

Skechers focuses heavily on immediate plushness. Their memory foam insoles feel like slippers the second you put them on. Puma shoes are generally firmer out of the box, designed to provide stability for sports and workouts.

2. Longevity & Durability: Tie

Both brands use quality materials like synthetic leather and mesh. However, Skechers' ultra-soft foam tends to compress and flatten after 1-2 years of heavy daily wear. Puma's firmer soles often retain their structure slightly longer.

3. Price & Value in India

Puma is the undisputed winner here. While both brands have high MRPs, Puma aggressively discounts their shoes on Amazon and Flipkart. You can frequently find excellent Puma sneakers under ₹1,500. Skechers rarely drops below ₹2,500.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better: Puma or Skechers?

It depends on your needs. Skechers is superior for pure walking comfort and plush memory foam. Puma is better for style, gym training, and athletic performance.

Are Skechers more comfortable than Puma?

For standing all day, yes. Skechers uses very soft, plush insoles (like their Arch Fit line) designed for walking. Puma shoes are usually firmer to provide better stability for sports.

Which brand is cheaper in India?

Both brands have frequent sales, but Skechers generally has a higher entry price point. Puma frequently offers massive discounts on Amazon and Flipkart, often bringing their lifestyle sneakers under ₹2,000.

Bottom Line

If you want style, versatility, and the best bank-for-your-buck, grab the Puma Smashic. If you suffer from foot pain or stand all day at work, invest the extra money in the Skechers Go Walk 7.
