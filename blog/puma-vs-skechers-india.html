Puma vs Skechers: Which is Better for Daily Wear in India? (2026)

Updated 8 October 2026 · Based on real prices tracked across Amazon, Flipkart & Myntra

If you're looking for a comfortable sneaker for daily wear in India, you've probably narrowed it down to Puma or Skechers. Both are popular, both are widely available, and both go on sale frequently.

But which one actually gives you more value? We track prices daily across Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra. So instead of vague opinions, here's a real breakdown based on price, comfort, and durability.

The Quick Verdict

Puma wins on value. Skechers wins on comfort.

If you want the best deal and a stylish sneaker, Puma is the clear winner. Their shoes discount aggressively, and the Flyer Flex at ₹1,499 is unbeatable value. If you stand all day or want maximum cushioning, Skechers is the better pick. Their memory foam insoles are genuinely more comfortable for long hours.

Round 1: Price & Discounts — Puma Wins

Puma is the most aggressive discounter in the Indian sneaker market. Their shoes routinely drop to 50-65% off on Amazon and Flipkart. The Puma Flyer Flex is currently 63% off (₹1,499 from ₹3,999), and the Melanite is 65% off (₹1,578 from ₹4,499).

Skechers also discounts well. The Equalizer 5.0 is 75% off (₹1,609 from ₹6,499), and the Modern Cool is 40% off (₹1,679 from ₹2,799). But Skechers' base prices are higher, so even at 40-50% off, their shoes cost more than an equivalent Puma.

Winner: Puma. Better entry price and consistent discounts.

Round 2: Comfort — Skechers Wins

This is Skechers' superpower. Their memory foam insoles are genuinely more comfortable than Puma's for standing and walking. If you have a job where you're on your feet 8+ hours (retail, hospitality, teaching), Skechers is a lifesaver.

Puma is more athletic. Their shoes are designed for running and gym use, so the cushioning is firmer and more responsive. For walking around casually, Puma is comfortable. For all-day standing, Skechers wins.

Winner: Skechers for pure comfort.

Round 3: Durability — Tie

Both brands have similar lifespans. Expect 12-18 months of daily wear from either Puma or Skechers before significant wear shows.

Skechers' memory foam tends to flatten faster (about 8-12 months of daily use), while Puma's mesh uppers tend to tear in the same timeframe. Neither is built to last 3+ years — for that, you'd need leather sneakers from Adidas or New balance.

Winner: Tie. Both last a year or so with daily use.

Round 4: Style — Puma Wins

Puma has invested heavily in design recently. Their Flyer Flex, Melanite, and Smashic models are trendy and fit modern streetwear. Puma is what college students and young professionals gravitate toward.

Skechers plays it safe. Their designs are functional but rarely fashionable. If you care about how your shoes look, Puma is the better pick.

Winner: Puma. More modern and stylish.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which brand is more comfortable, Puma or Skechers?

Skechers is more comfortable for standing and walking all day, thanks to their memory foam insoles. Puma is more comfortable for running and athletic activities because of their firmer, more responsive cushioning.

Is Puma or Skechers better value in India?

Puma offers better value for money. Their shoes are cheaper at full price, discount more aggressively, and often look better. Skechers gives you superior comfort for a slightly higher price.

Which brand lasts longer?

Both last around 12-18 months with daily use. Neither is significantly more durable than the other. If you want a longer-lasting sneaker, spend a bit more on Adidas leather sneakers like the VS Pace 2.0.

Where is the cheapest place to buy Puma and Skechers in India?

It varies by product. Right now, Amazon has the lowest prices on most Puma and Skechers sneakers, but Flipkart runs better deals during festival sales. Always check all three stores before buying — that's exactly what StyleScout does.

Bottom Line

There's no universal winner — it depends on what you want. Buy Puma if you want the best value, more style, and a sneaker that works for both casual wear and light sports. Buy Skechers if you want maximum comfort for standing and walking all day.
