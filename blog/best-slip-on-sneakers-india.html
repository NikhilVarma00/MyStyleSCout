Frequently Asked Questions

Are slip-on sneakers comfortable for daily wear?

Absolutely. Slip-on sneakers are incredibly convenient and many, like the Campus Oxyfit, feature memory foam insoles that make them perfect for all-day walking and running errands.

Can I wear slip-on shoes to the office?

Yes, especially loafers or formal slip-on sneakers. The Red Tape Formal Slip-On offers a sleek look that pairs perfectly with chinos or formal trousers.

Do slip-on shoes stretch over time?

Yes, most slip-on shoes made of canvas, mesh, or leather will stretch slightly and mold to the shape of your foot after a few wears.

Bottom Line

For maximum comfort, grab the Campus Oxyfit. For a slightly more premium daily shoe, the Red Tape Walking Slip-On is the way to go.
