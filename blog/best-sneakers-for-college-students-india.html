What to Look For in a College Sneaker

College is tough on shoes. Here's what to prioritize:

  • Cushioned sole — your feet will thank you after long walks
  • Neutral color — white, black, or grey pairs with everything
  • Durable upper — mesh or leather, not cheap canvas
  • Correct size — always size up by 0.5 if buying ASIAN or Sparx

Frequently Asked Questions

Which sneakers are trending for college students in India?

Mid-top sneakers with printed or color-blocked designs are very popular right now (like the ASIAN Nexus-11). Clean white sneakers are also an evergreen trend. Chunky "dad shoe" silhouettes are growing in popularity too.

How long should a college sneaker last?

A good pair under ₹1,000 should last 8-12 months with daily wear. If you buy a Puma or Adidas, expect 1.5-2 years. Rotating between two pairs extends their life significantly.

Where is the cheapest place to buy sneakers for college?

It varies by product. Right now, Amazon has the lowest prices on most sub-₹1,000 sneakers, but Flipkart runs better deals during sales. Always check all three stores before buying — that's exactly what StyleScout does.
