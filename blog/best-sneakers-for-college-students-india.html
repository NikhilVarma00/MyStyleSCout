Frequently Asked Questions

Which sneakers are best for college students in India?

The best sneakers for college are ones that balance style, comfort, and price. Brands like Campus, Sparx, and Red Tape offer excellent options under ₹1000 that look great and survive daily wear.

Are white sneakers good for college?

Yes, white sneakers are highly versatile and pair well with almost any outfit. However, they require regular cleaning to maintain their look. Consider a white shoe with black or blue accents to hide minor scuffs.

How long do budget sneakers last?

With regular wear, budget sneakers from brands like Sparx or Campus typically last 12 to 18 months. Proper maintenance, like avoiding machine washing and keeping them dry, can extend their lifespan.

Bottom Line

You don't need to burn a hole in your pocket to look good in college. Grab the Sparx SM 323 for the best budget option, or the Campus Oxyfit if you want maximum comfort for those long walks across campus.
