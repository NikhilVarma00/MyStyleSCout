The Problem with Buying Sneakers Online

Different platforms run different sales. Amazon might have a deal on Puma, while Myntra is running a Buy 2 Get 1 Free event, and Flipkart has a massive bank discount. If you only check one app, you're almost certainly missing out on the best deal.

How to Compare Sneaker Prices (The Right Way)

1. Check the "Real" Price, Not the MRP

A shoe might have an MRP of ₹4,999, but the current selling price is ₹1,499. Always look at the final checkout price after applying any available coupons or bank offers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do sneaker prices fluctuate so much?

E-commerce platforms use dynamic pricing algorithms. Prices change based on demand, stock levels, and competitor pricing. A shoe might cost ₹1,000 more on Myntra than on Amazon on any given day.

How does StyleScout compare prices?

StyleScout tracks live pricing data across Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra. We update our catalog to show you the lowest available price so you never overpay.

Are cheaper prices always better?

Not always. Consider bank offers, shipping times, and return policies. Sometimes a slightly higher price on Myntra is better if you have a Myntra coupon or faster delivery in your area.
