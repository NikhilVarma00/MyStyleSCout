Why Does the Same Sneaker Cost Different Prices?

Every platform runs on its own pricing algorithm. Here's why the gap can be huge:

  • Inventory Clearance: If Amazon has too much stock of a Nike model, they drop the price to clear it. Flipkart might still be selling it at full MRP.
  • Bank Offers: Flipkart might offer a 10% instant discount with HDFC cards, making it cheaper than Amazon — even if the listed price is higher.
  • Seller Competition: On Amazon and Flipkart, multiple third-party sellers list the same shoe. One seller might undercut the other by ₹500.
  • Platform Exclusives: Some models are exclusive to Myntra or Ajio, so they can charge whatever they want.

The lesson: Never buy a sneaker without checking at least 3 platforms first.

Step 3: Check Bank Offers

Before you click "Buy Now", check if your bank has an instant discount on that platform. A ₹2,000 shoe with a 10% HDFC discount is effectively ₹1,800.

Step 4: Set a Price Alert

If you're not in a hurry, wait for a sale. Prices typically drop during Amazon Prime Day, Flipkart Big Billion Days, and Myntra End of Reason Sale.
