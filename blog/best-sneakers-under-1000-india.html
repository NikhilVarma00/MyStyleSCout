What to Look For in Sneakers Under ₹1000

Operating in the ultra-budget tier requires keeping core selection principles in mind:

  • Thermal Ventilation: Opt for mesh-heavy uppers to ensure proper airflow in warm weather.
  • Outsole Integrity: EVA and rubber compounds offer dependable traction and shock absorption.
  • Accurate Sizing: Domestic value brands (like Sparx and ASIAN) often feature tapered fits; always verify size charts.
  • Intended Application: Match footwear specifically to your needs—avoid using casual lifestyle shoes for intense distance running.
