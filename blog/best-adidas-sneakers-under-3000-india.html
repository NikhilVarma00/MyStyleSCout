Frequently Asked Questions

Which Adidas sneaker is best under ₹3000?

The Adidas Lite Racer 4.0 is a fantastic everyday sneaker. For running and training, the Adidas Air-Fleetfly or Cloud Tec M offer the best value under ₹2000.

Are Adidas shoes good for daily wear?

Yes, Adidas lifestyle and running shoes are highly durable and comfortable. Their Cloudfoam midsole provides excellent cushioning for daily use.

Do Adidas shoes run true to size?

Generally, yes. However, Adidas running shoes like the Runfalcon can feel slightly narrow, so people with wider feet often size up by half a size.

Bottom Line

You can't go wrong with the Adidas Air-Fleetfly for running or the Adidas Cloud Tec M for a budget everyday shoe. Both offer the quality you expect from Adidas at a fraction of the price.
