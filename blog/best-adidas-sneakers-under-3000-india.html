7. How to choose the right Adidas sneaker

Start with how you'll use it

Decide whether you need a casual sneaker, footwear for general daily use or a shoe specifically intended for running. A model that looks sporty isn't necessarily designed for every sporting activity.

Check the exact product listing

Read the description for the exact model and version. Check the material details, size chart, available colours and care instructions. Don't assume that two Adidas models have the same fit or construction.

Compare the final checkout price

A displayed product price may not include every delivery charge or other applicable fee. Check the seller, delivery details, return conditions and final payable amount before ordering.

Don't choose only by discount

A large discount does not automatically make a sneaker the best choice. Prioritise the intended use, fit, product details and seller reliability over the size of a claimed discount.

8. Before buying: a quick checklist

  • Confirm that the listing is for the exact Adidas model you want.
  • Check your size against the seller's size chart.
  • Compare the available colour and version.
  • Review seller information, delivery charges and return conditions.
  • Check the final price at retailer checkout before paying.
  • For running, choose a model whose stated purpose matches your needs.
