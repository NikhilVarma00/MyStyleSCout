Frequently Asked Questions

Is Campus a good brand for sneakers?

Yes, Campus is one of the best budget brands in India. They are especially known for their memory foam walking shoes (like the Oxyfit) and stylish, durable casual sneakers.

Which Campus sneaker is best for running?

The Campus Cyron and Campus Swoon are solid entry-level running shoes. They offer decent cushioning and breathability for daily jogs without breaking the bank.

Are Campus shoes true to size?

Generally, yes. However, some users find the fit slightly snug. If you have wide feet, it is recommended to go half a size up, especially for their slip-on models.

Bottom Line

You don't need to spend a fortune to get a great pair of shoes. If you want ultimate comfort, grab the Campus Oxyfit. If you need something for the gym, the Campus Cyron is a fantastic budget-friendly runner.
