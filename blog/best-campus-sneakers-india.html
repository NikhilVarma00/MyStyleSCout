Frequently Asked Questions

Are Campus sneakers good quality?

Yes, for the price. Campus sneakers are a solid choice under ₹1,500. They're not as durable as Puma or Adidas, but they offer excellent value and comfort for the money. Expect 8–12 months of daily use.

Which is the best Campus sneaker for running?

The Campus Terminator is their best running shoe. For serious runners, the Campus Cyron is also excellent. Both have cushioned soles and durable outsoles for 5–10 km runs.

Are Campus sneakers true to size?

Campus sneakers run slightly small. If you're between sizes, size up by 0.5. If you have wide feet, always size up by 1.

Where is the cheapest place to buy Campus sneakers in India?

It varies by product. Right now, Amazon has the lowest prices on most Campus sneakers, but Flipkart runs better deals during sales. Always check all three stores before buying — that's exactly what StyleScout does.
