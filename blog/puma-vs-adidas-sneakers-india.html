Puma vs Adidas: Which Sneakers Are Better Value in India? (2026)

Updated 6 October 2026 · Based on real prices tracked across Amazon, Flipkart & Myntra

Walk into any college Campus, gym, or office in India, and you'll see the same two logos everywhere: Puma and Adidas. Both are German giants with excellent reputations, but they offer very different value depending on what you're looking for.

We track prices daily across Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra. So instead of vague opinions, we're comparing the two brands based on real prices, actual durability, and what you get for your money.

The Quick Verdict

Puma wins on value. Adidas wins on durability.

If you want the best sneaker under ₹2,000, Puma is the clear winner. They discount aggressively, and their budget models (Flyer Flex, Melanite) offer more tech and style per rupee. If you're spending ₹2,500+ and want a shoe that lasts 2+ years, Adidas is the better investment.

Round 1: Price & Discounts — Puma Wins

Puma is the most aggressive discounter of the two in India. Their shoes routinely drop to 50-65% off on Amazon and Flipkart. For example, the Puma Flyer Flex usually retails for ₹3,999, but we found it for ₹1,499 (63% off). The Puma Melanite drops from ₹4,499 to ₹1,578.

Adidas does run discounts, but they're smaller. The Adidas VS Pace 2.0 drops from ₹3,959 to ₹1,690 (57% off), but their newer releases (like Grand Court 2.0) rarely go below 10-15% off.

Winner: Puma. You'll find bigger, more frequent discounts.

Round 2: Durability — Adidas Wins

Puma makes comfortable, lightweight shoes. But if you're hard on your sneakers (daily college use, rough gym sessions, monsoon wear), they tend to break down faster. The mesh uppers on Puma sneakers tear after 8-12 months of heavy use.

Adidas sneakers, especially the leather ones like the VS Pace 2.0 and Grand Court 2.0, last longer. The leather upper holds its shape, the sole doesn't flatten as quickly, and they survive monsoon much better.

Winner: Adidas. Leather > mesh for long-term durability in India.

Round 3: Comfort — Tie

Puma is better for all-day comfort. Their mesh uppers are more breathable, and the soft foam insoles are forgiving for long walks (great for college students).

Adidas is better for activity-specific comfort. Their running shoes (Runfalcon 3.0, Air-Fleetfly) have firmer, more supportive soles that reduce fatigue during long runs or gym sessions.

Winner: Tie. Puma for casual wear, Adidas for sports.

Round 4: Style — Depends on Your Vibe

Puma's current lineup is trendier. The chunky sneaker trend, the slip-on Melanite, and the sporty Flyer Flex all fit modern streetwear. Puma is what college students and young professionals gravitate toward.

Adidas plays the classics: the Samba, Gazelle, Superstar, and Grand Court. Their silhouettes are timeless. If you want a shoe that looks good 5 years from now, Adidas is safer.

Winner: Depends on your personal style.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which brand is cheaper in India, Puma or Adidas?

Puma is generally cheaper for the same category. Puma sneakers regularly drop below ₹1,500, while similar Adidas models usually stay above ₹1,600 even during sales.

Is Puma or Adidas better for the gym?

Adidas is slightly better for heavy lifting because their soles are firmer. Puma is better for cardio and treadmill work because their mesh is more breathable and lighter.

Which brand lasts longer?

Adidas lasts longer, especially their leather sneakers. Puma's mesh sneakers are lighter and more breathable but tend to wear out faster with daily heavy use.

Bottom Line

There's no single "better" brand — it depends on what you want. Puma gives you more style and tech for less money, making it the best choice under ₹2,000. Adidas gives you durability and timeless design, making it the better long-term investment above ₹2,500.
