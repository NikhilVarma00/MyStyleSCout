Skechers vs New Balance: Which is Better? (2026)

Updated 2 November 2026 · Prices tracked across Amazon, Flipkart & Myntra

When you're looking for a comfortable, reliable pair of shoes in India, Skechers and New Balance usually end up on your shortlist. Both brands have massive followings, but they approach footwear from completely different angles.

Skechers is the king of immediate, out-of-the-box plush comfort. New Balance is the heritage athletic brand built for support, stability, and wide-foot comfort. So, which one should you actually spend your money on? We compared live prices, durability, and comfort to give you the definitive answer.

The Quick Verdict

Choose Skechers if your priority is walking, standing all day, or you want a super-soft, memory-foam feel from day one. They are generally more affordable.

Choose New Balance if you need serious athletic support, have wide feet, or want better long-term durability and retro style (like the BB550).

Comfort & Durability Breakdown

1. Initial Comfort: Skechers Wins

Skechers is famous for its "memory foam" or "air-cooled" insoles. When you first put them on, they feel like slippers. However, this soft foam tends to compress and flatten over time. New Balance shoes are usually firmer out of the box but offer better structural support, which keeps your feet from getting tired during long walks.

2. Longevity & Durability: New Balance Wins

This is where New Balance pulls away. New Balance uses premium suede, pigskin, and high-quality mesh on their lifestyle shoes. They also use their proprietary midsole technology, which retains its bounce far longer than Skechers' softer foams. Skechers are great, but they usually need replacing faster if worn daily.

3. Price & Value in India

Skechers is the clear winner if you are on a strict budget. Most Skechers models range from ₹2,500 to ₹5,000. New Balance is priced at a premium in India, with lifestyle models rarely dropping below ₹5,000, and some collaborations crossing ₹15,000.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better, Skechers or New Balance?

Skechers is better for casual walking and immediate plush comfort. New Balance is better for athletic support, wide-foot comfort, and long-term durability.

Are New Balance shoes worth the price in India?

Yes. While they cost more, the premium materials and proprietary midsole technology make them last longer and provide better support than budget alternatives.

Bottom Line

If you want to feel like you're walking on clouds right out of the box, go with Skechers. If you want a durable, stylish shoe that will support your feet for years, spend the extra money on New Balance.
