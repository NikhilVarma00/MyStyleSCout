Are Skechers Good for Running? Honest Review for Indian Runners (2026)

Updated 8 October 2026 · Prices tracked across Amazon, Flipkart & Myntra

Skechers is one of the most popular sneaker brands in India. Walk into any mall and you'll see their stores. But here's the question every runner asks: Are Skechers actually good for running?

Short answer: It depends on which Skechers you buy. Some models are genuinely great running shoes. Others are just comfortable walking shoes dressed up to look athletic. Here's the honest breakdown.

The Quick Verdict

For casual jogging and 5K runs: Yes. For serious 10K+ training: No.

Skechers makes excellent shoes for the gym, treadmills, and casual runs. But if you're training seriously for a marathon or doing 40+ km a week, you should look at dedicated running brands like Nike, Adidas, Asics, or Brooks. Skechers is a comfort brand first, a running brand second.

The Good: Where Skechers Actually Works

  • Excellent cushioning — Skechers' Memory Foam and Hyper Burst foam are extremely comfortable, especially for beginners.
  • Great for treadmills — For gym runs and treadmill work, Skechers performs very well.
  • Comfortable for long walks — Perfect if you're walking 10+ km a day for work or travel.
  • Great discounts — Skechers routinely goes 40-75% off in India, giving you premium shoes for budget prices.

The Bad: Where Skechers Falls Short

  • Not for high-mileage running — Foam flattens after 400-600 km of running. Dedicated running shoes last 800-1,000 km.
  • Poor for road running — The outsoles aren't designed for Indian roads (potholes, uneven surfaces).
  • Limited stability — Skechers doesn't offer proper stability shoes for overpronators.
  • Heavy — Skechers shoes are typically heavier than equivalent Nike or Adidas running shoes.

So Should You Buy Skechers for Running?

  • ✅ Buy Skechers if: You do casual 3K-5K runs, treadmill sessions, or walk a lot. You value comfort over performance.
  • ❌ Skip Skechers if: You're training for a marathon, running on Indian roads daily, or doing 40+ km per week.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Skechers good for running on Indian roads?

Not ideal. Indian roads are uneven, and Skechers' outsoles are thin and designed for smooth surfaces. For Indian road running, Nike or Adidas running shoes are better.

Which Skechers model is best for running?

The Skechers GO Run line (specifically the Go Run Ride) is the best for actual running. The Summits Brisbane and Edgeride are also solid options for beginner runners.

Are Skechers better than Nike for walking?

Yes, for pure walking, Skechers is better. Their Memory Foam insoles are more comfortable for 8+ hour standing and walking than Nike's running-focused designs.

Do Skechers last long?

For walking: 1-2 years. For running: 8-12 months with daily use. The Memory Foam flattens after about a year, so expect to replace them.

Bottom Line

Skechers is a good brand, but it's not the best running brand in India. For casual runners and walkers, it's excellent. For serious runners, stick to Nike, Adidas, or Asics. Our top running pick from Skechers is the Summits Brisbane at ₹2,060 for beginners, and the Edgeride for more experienced runners.
