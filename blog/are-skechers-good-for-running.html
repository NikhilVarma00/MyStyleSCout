Are Skechers Good for Running? (2026 Review)

Updated 4 November 2026 · Prices tracked across Amazon, Flipkart & Myntra

Skechers is a massive brand in India, famous for its incredibly comfortable walking shoes and memory foam insoles. But if you walk into a Skechers store, you'll see shelves full of shoes that look like running shoes. The big question is: Should you actually run in them?

Here is the no-BS answer: It depends on what kind of running you are doing. We broke down the science, the models, and the prices to help you decide.

The Quick Verdict

Yes, for casual jogging and the treadmill. Skechers offers great cushioning and comfort for beginners.

No, for marathons or serious road running. Their ultra-soft soles lack the required energy return, stability, and durability for high-mileage runners.

Why Skechers is Great for Walking (But Not Always Running)

Skechers built their empire on the "Go Walk" line. These shoes use ultra-soft memory foam and flexible mesh uppers that feel like slippers. For walking 10,000 steps a day, they are phenomenal.

However, running involves up to 3x your body weight in impact force with every stride. If a shoe is too soft, your foot sinks into it, causing your arch to collapse and your knees to take the brunt of the shock. Skechers' softest shoes simply don't offer the firm, springy energy return that brands like Nike, Adidas, or ASICS provide for runners.

When Should You Avoid Skechers for Running?

  • You are running over 10km: The foam will compress and lose its bounce after a few kilometers.
  • You need arch support: Unless you buy their specific "Arch Fit" running line, most Skechers are neutral and soft.
  • You are running on uneven roads: The soft mesh uppers don't offer great lateral stability for uneven pavement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Skechers good for running?

Skechers are excellent for walking and casual jogging, but they are generally not recommended for serious long-distance running or marathons. Their soles are usually too soft and flexible for high-performance running.

Which Skechers model is best for running?

The Skechers Summits and Edgeride series offer better stability and cushioning for light running and treadmill use compared to their Go Walk line.

Can I use walking shoes for running?

You can, but it's not ideal. Walking shoes lack the specific shock absorption and forward-roll geometry that running shoes have, which can lead to foot fatigue or injury over time.

Bottom Line

If you want a Skechers shoe for the gym or light jogging, grab the Skechers Summits Brisbane. If you just want ultimate walking comfort, stick to the Go Walk line.
