Campus vs Sparx: Which Budget Sneaker Brand Wins in India? (2026)

Updated 6 October 2026 · Based on real prices tracked across Amazon, Flipkart & Myntra

When you're shopping for sneakers under ₹1,500 in India, you almost always end up choosing between two brands: Campus and Sparx. Both are Indian, both are affordable, and both are everywhere — Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, and local shoe stores.

But which one actually gives you better value? We track prices daily across all three major stores. So instead of vague opinions, we're comparing them on real prices, actual durability, and what you get for your money.

The Quick Verdict

Sparx wins on durability. Campus wins on style.

If you want a shoe that lasts 1+ years of daily college or gym use, Sparx is the safer bet. Their rubber soles are more durable, and the SM series is built like a tank. If you want a shoe that looks good and feels soft right out of the box, Campus is the better pick. Their Oxyfit and Terminator models are genuinely comfortable.

Round 1: Price & Value — Sparx Wins

Sparx has the edge here. Their shoes routinely drop below ₹700, and the SM-323 is currently available for just ₹629. Campus shoes usually start around ₹580–₹650, but their popular models (like Terminator or Dripp) climb above ₹1,200 quickly.

If pure budget is your priority, Sparx gives you more sneaker per rupee at the very bottom of the price range.

Winner: Sparx for the lowest entry price.

Round 2: Durability — Sparx Wins

Sparx sneakers are built to last. The rubber soles don't flatten easily, and the stitching holds up well against daily wear. If you're walking 5+ km a day or using them for gym sessions, Sparx will outlast Campus by 3–6 months.

Campus sneakers are more comfortable initially but tend to wear out faster. The cushioning flattens after 6–8 months of heavy use, and the mesh uppers tear more easily.

Winner: Sparx. Better long-term value for heavy users.

Round 3: Comfort — Campus Wins

Campus wins this round easily. Their Oxyfit and Brisk models have softer cushioning that feels great from day one. If you have wide feet or need extra arch support, Campus is more forgiving.

Sparx sneakers are firmer. That's great for gym work and running (firm soles provide better stability), but for all-day walking, they can feel stiff at first.

Winner: Campus. Softer, more comfortable for long days.

Round 4: Style — Campus Wins

Campus has invested heavily in design over the last few years. The Oxyfit, Dripp, and OG series all feature modern, streetwear-inspired silhouettes. The Nexus-11 and Campus OG-27 are genuinely trendy.

Sparx plays it safe. Their designs are classic, clean, and casual. They look fine, but they don't stand out. If you want to make a statement with your sneakers, Campus is the better pick.

Winner: Campus. More modern, more stylish.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which brand is better, Campus or Sparx?

It depends on what you value. Campus is better for comfort and style, especially if you want trendy sneakers. Sparx is better for durability and value, especially if you're hard on your shoes.

Are Campus sneakers good quality?

Yes, for the price. Campus sneakers are a solid choice under ₹1,500. They're not as durable as branded shoes like Puma or Adidas, but they offer excellent value and comfort for the money.

Which brand is more comfortable, Campus or Sparx?

Campus is generally more comfortable. Their cushioning is softer and more forgiving for all-day wear. Sparx offers better support but feels firmer until they're broken in.

Bottom Line

There's no clear "winner" — it depends on what you want. Campus gives you better style and comfort, making it ideal for college students and casual wearers. Sparx gives you better durability and value, making it the smarter pick if you need a shoe that survives heavy daily use.
