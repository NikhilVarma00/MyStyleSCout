Campus vs Sparx: Which is Better in 2026?

A practical StyleScout buying guide · Updated 25 October 2026

If you are shopping for budget sneakers in India, two heavyweights dominate the conversation: Campus and Sparx. Both homegrown brands offer phenomenal affordability, wide availability, and huge catalogs of casual and athletic footwear.

Yet, when standing in a retail store or staring down an e-commerce listing trying to choose between "Campus or Sparx," the right pick isn't always obvious. They cater to different types of everyday wearers.

The Quick Verdict

Choose Campus if you prioritize all-day walking comfort, plush memory foam cushioning, and trendy, modern casual aesthetics.

Choose Sparx if you want rock-bottom pricing, an ultra-lightweight feel for the gym, or rugged, sport-inspired designs.

1. Price Comparison: Which Brand is Cheaper?

Both brands sit comfortably in the budget segment, but Sparx generally undercuts Campus by a couple of hundred rupees across baseline models.

  • Sparx: Everyday casuals and running variants like the Sparx SM 323 or SM-439 frequently sell between ₹600 and ₹900.
  • Campus: Standard walking shoes and stylish lifestyle sneakers typically range from ₹900 to ₹1,400.

Keep an eye out during mega e-commerce festivals (like Amazon Great Indian Festival or Flipkart Big Billion Days). Price differences often narrow down to mere pocket change during bank discount windows, making it essential to check live listings before checking out.

2. Comfort & Cushioning: Campus Takes the Crown

If comfort is your primary concern, Campus wins. Many of their top-selling shoes (such as the Campus Oxyfit series) incorporate memory foam insoles and forgiving midsoles. If your daily routine involves standing for hours or long walks across college or office campuses, Campus keeps foot fatigue at bay.

Sparx footwear tends to be lighter and more breathable—making them fantastic for warm weather or intense gym sessions—but their soles are characteristically firmer and offer less plush cushioning out of the box.

3. Durability & Materials: It's a Tie

At sub-₹1,500 price points, material quality is largely uniform. Both brands rely heavily on breathable synthetic mesh uppers, durable PU overlays, and EVA or Phylon soles.

Aggregated customer reviews across leading e-commerce platforms show that both brands hold up for roughly 12 to 18 months of regular wear. Common points of wear include mesh stretching near the toe box or sole compression over time. Ultimately, regular cleaning, proper drying, and shoe rotation will extend the lifespan of either brand far more than brand choice alone.

4. Style & Design: Sparx is Sportier, Campus is Casual

Aesthetic preferences vary, but distinct design philosophies separate the two:

  • Campus: Focuses heavily on modern chunky silhouettes, athleisure trends, and versatile color blocking that pairs effortlessly with denim and joggers.
  • Sparx: Leans into rugged, athletic, and performance-inspired lines. They excel if you want a bold, sporty look suited for track pants and activewear.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better, Campus or Sparx?

Campus takes the lead for all-day walking comfort thanks to its signature memory foam. Sparx excels for users seeking lightweight, athletic styling at an even lower price point.

Are Campus shoes more expensive than Sparx?

Generally, yes. Campus shoes typically range from ₹900 to ₹1,500, whereas Sparx leans slightly lower at ₹600 to ₹1,200, though sales frequently close the gap.

Do Campus shoes last longer than Sparx?

In this budget category, durability is comparable. With routine use, you can expect 12 to 18 months of reliable service from either brand.

Are Sparx shoes good for running?

Sparx offers great lightweight options for gym workouts, jogging, and walking, but they aren't engineered for professional marathon or long-distance running training.
