Nike Revolution 7 Review: Is It Worth ₹3,000 in India? (2026)

Comfort: 8/10

The Revolution 7 has Nike's signature soft foam underfoot. If you're a beginner, walking around the block, or doing 5K jogs, this cushioning feels great. It's plush and forgiving.

However, it's not designed for high-mileage runners. If you're doing 10K+ regularly, the foam will compress and lose its bounce within 3-4 months.

Durability: 6/10

This is where the Revolution 7 loses points. The outsole rubber is thin, and the mesh upper can tear if you're rough with your shoes. Expect around 6-8 months of daily use before it starts showing significant wear.

Compare this to the Adidas VS Pace 2.0 (which uses leather), and the difference in lifespan is clear. For the same price, Adidas lasts longer.

Value for Money: 7/10

At full price (₹3,326), the Revolution 7 is overpriced. At ₹2,956 (its current lowest price), it's decent. But if you wait for a sale, you can sometimes find it below ₹2,500, which is when it becomes a genuinely good deal.

Who Should Buy the Nike Revolution 7?

  • ✅ Buy it if: You're new to running, you walk a lot, or you want a comfortable Nike shoe for gym/casual use.
  • ❌ Skip it if: You run more than 20km a week, you need a durable shoe, or you have wide feet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Nike Revolution 7 good for running?

Yes, for beginners running short distances (up to 5K). For longer distances, a more premium shoe like the Nike Downshifter 13 or a Puma running shoe is better.

How long does the Nike Revolution 7 last?

With daily use, expect 6-8 months. With light use (3 times a week), it can last up to a year.

Is the Nike Revolution 7 true to size?

It runs slightly narrow. If you have wide feet, we recommend ordering a half size up.

Bottom Line

The Nike Revolution 7 is a comfortable, lightweight, budget-friendly shoe for beginners. But it's not the best value in India. If you want durability for the same price, buy the Adidas VS Pace 2.0 (₹1,690) instead. If you want the best cushioning under ₹2,000, get the Puma Flyer Flex (₹1,499).

But if you want a Nike, and you can find it under ₹2,800, the Revolution 7 is a solid choice.
