Amazon Great Indian Festival: How to Find the Best Sneaker Deals

Updated 14 October 2026 · Sneaker shopping guide

Festival sales can be a good opportunity to shop for sneakers, but a large discount label does not automatically mean you are getting the best deal. Prices, available sizes, seller offers and delivery charges can differ between retailers, so it is worth comparing the complete offer before placing an order.

If you are planning to shop during the Amazon Great Indian Festival, this guide will help you compare sneaker options, evaluate discounts and avoid common buying mistakes. We have also included a few models you can explore through StyleScout.

1. Compare sneaker prices before buying

A sneaker may be advertised at a discount on one shopping platform, but that does not mean it is automatically the cheapest option available. The same model may have different listings, sellers, sizes and offers across Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra.

Before purchasing, compare the available listings for the exact model. Check the final amount, not just the percentage discount shown on the product page.

3. What to check before buying sneakers during a sale

A good purchase is about more than the discount. Use this checklist before placing your order:

  • Correct model: Make sure the product name and model number match the shoe you actually want.
  • Size availability: Confirm that your preferred size is available in the selected colour.
  • Seller details: Check who is selling the product and review the seller information on the retailer's website.
  • Final checkout price: Include delivery charges, applicable fees and any conditions attached to coupons or bank offers.
  • Return policy: Read the retailer's current return, exchange and refund conditions.
  • Product suitability: Check the manufacturer's specifications and choose footwear that suits your intended use.

4. How to avoid misleading sneaker discounts

Sale pages often highlight a percentage discount against a listed original or maximum retail price. That figure alone does not tell you whether the current offer is competitive.

Keep these points in mind:

  • Compare the same model rather than two shoes that only look similar.
  • Check the current selling price across more than one retailer.
  • Do not assume every advertised coupon or bank offer applies to you.
  • Check whether your size has a different price from other sizes.
  • Review the final checkout amount before completing the purchase.

A lower advertised price is useful only when the product, seller, size and final checkout conditions work for you.

Frequently asked questions

When does the Amazon Great Indian Festival take place?

Amazon announces its Great Indian Festival dates and sale conditions separately for each edition. Check Amazon's official website or app for the current sale dates, early-access details and offer conditions.

Not necessarily. Offers vary by model, size, seller and time. Compare the current selling price with other available listings and check the final checkout amount before deciding.
