Nike Revolution 7 vs Downshifter 13: Which Should You Buy in India? (2026)

Updated 6 October 2026 · Prices tracked across Amazon, Flipkart & Myntra

If you're looking for a budget Nike running shoe in India, you've probably narrowed it down to two options: the Nike Revolution 7 and the Nike Downshifter 13. Both are excellent, both are priced similarly, and both have the iconic Nike swoosh.

So what's the actual difference? We track prices daily across Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra. Here's a real breakdown of which one is right for you.

The Quick Verdict

Beginners & walkers: Choose the Revolution 7. Regular runners: Choose the Downshifter 13.

The Nike Revolution 7 has softer foam, making it more comfortable for walking, gym use, and beginner runs. The Nike Downshifter 13 is slightly firmer and more structured, making it better for regular 5K-10K runners who need more support.

Round 1: Price — Revolution 7 Wins

The Revolution 7 is the more affordable option. It's currently available for around ₹2,956 (down from ₹3,326). The Downshifter 13 usually stays closer to ₹4,295 and discounts less frequently.

If budget is your main concern, the Revolution 7 gives you more Nike for less money.

Winner: Nike Revolution 7.

Round 2: Comfort & Cushioning — Revolution 7 Wins

The Revolution 7 has softer foam underfoot. If you're going to be standing or walking for 8+ hours, or if you're new to running, this extra cushioning makes a real difference. It feels plush and forgiving from day one.

The Downshifter 13 is firmer. That's intentional — firmer foam provides better energy return for runners who are putting in real miles. But for casual use, it can feel stiff at first.

Winner: Nike Revolution 7 for casual comfort. Downshifter 13 for performance running.

Round 3: Durability — Downshifter 13 Wins

The Downshifter 13 has a more durable outsole and a slightly sturdier upper. If you're running 3-4 times a week, the Downshifter will last longer than the Revolution 7.

The Revolution 7 is still good, but the foam tends to flatten faster under heavy use. If you're a heavy runner or use your shoes roughly, the Downshifter is the better long-term investment.

Winner: Nike Downshifter 13.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Nike Revolution 7 good for running?

Yes, for beginners and casual runners doing short distances (up to 5K). For longer distances or regular training, the Downshifter 13 or a more premium Nike model is better.

Is Nike Downshifter 13 better than Revolution 7?

It depends on your use case. The Downshifter 13 is better for serious runners who need durability. The Revolution 7 is better for casual wear and beginners who want more cushioning.

Which is more comfortable, Revolution 7 or Downshifter 13?

The Revolution 7 is more comfortable for walking and standing all day. The Downshifter 13 is more comfortable for running because of its firmer, more supportive ride.

Bottom Line

If you want a comfortable, affordable Nike sneaker for walking, gym, and casual runs, get the Nike Revolution 7. If you're a regular runner who needs a durable, supportive shoe for 5K-10K runs, spend the extra on the Nike Downshifter 13.
