Frequently Asked Questions

Which running shoe is best under ₹2000?

The Adidas Air-Fleetfly is the best overall pick under ₹2000. For a budget option, the Sparx SM-171 offers great cushioning, and the Campus Cyron is excellent for daily gym runs.

Are cheap running shoes bad for your feet?

Not necessarily. Brands like Adidas, Campus, and Sparx offer decent arch support and cushioning in the ₹1000-₹2000 range. However, for marathons or high-mileage training, you may need to invest in premium shoes.
