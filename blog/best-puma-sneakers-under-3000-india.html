Which is the best Puma sneaker under ₹3000 in India?

The Puma Flyer Flex is the best overall value at ₹1,499. If you want something more stylish, the Puma Melanite at ₹1,578 is the best-looking option. For casual daily wear, the Puma Smashic is the most comfortable.

Yes, Puma offers excellent quality for the price, especially when you catch a sale. Their mesh sneakers are lightweight and breathable, while their leather options are more durable. They're not as long-lasting as Nike or Adidas at full price, but at 50–65% off, they're unbeatable value.
