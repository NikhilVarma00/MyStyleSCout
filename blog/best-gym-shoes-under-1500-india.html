Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need special shoes for the gym?

Not necessarily. A lightweight, breathable sneaker equipped with a stable sole accommodates 90% of standard gym routines. However, if your program centers heavily around heavy barbell squats or deadlifts, flat-soled footwear provides superior ground stability.

Are ₹500 gym shoes good enough?

Yes, entry-level models suffice for casual walking, light stretching, and basic treadmill warmups. For demanding HIIT sessions or frequent weight training, investing in the ₹800–₹1,200 tier guarantees better seam reinforcement and structural support.

Can I wear running shoes to the gym?

Certainly. Running shoes perform admirably on treadmills, stationary bikes, and upright cable machines. While their cushioned heels aren't optimized for heavy powerlifting or leg press stabilization, they serve general fitness purposes well.
