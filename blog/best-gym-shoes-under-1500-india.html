What to Look For in a Budget Gym Shoe

At this price, you can't expect premium materials. But you should expect:

  • Breathable mesh upper — keeps feet cool during sweaty workouts
  • EVA or rubber sole — for grip and shock absorption
  • Lightweight build — heavy shoes make cardio harder
  • Correct size — always size up by 0.5 if buying ASIAN or Sparx

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need special shoes for the gym?

Not necessarily. A lightweight, breathable sneaker with a cushioned sole works for 90% of gym activities. If you're doing heavy squats or deadlifts, a flat-soled shoe like Converse is actually better.

Are ₹500 gym shoes good enough?

Yes, for basic treadmill runs, walking, and light lifting. If you do intense HIIT or heavy lifting, spend ₹800–1,200 for better durability and support.

Can I wear running shoes to the gym?

Yes. Running shoes are perfectly fine for treadmills, cycling, and most machines. They're not ideal for heavy lifting or exercises like leg press, but they work for general fitness.
