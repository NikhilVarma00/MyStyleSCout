What Beginners Should Look For in a Running Shoe

When you're starting out, don't overthink it. Focus on these 4 things:

  • Cushioning — You want a soft, plush midsole. Running on hard surfaces (Indian roads) is brutal on your joints.
  • Breathability — Mesh uppers keep your feet cool and dry during runs. Avoid leather for running.
  • Correct size — Your running shoe should be 0.5 size bigger than your casual shoe. Your feet swell during runs.
  • Genuine discount — Don't pay MRP. Check prices across Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra before buying.

How to Start Running (Without Getting Injured)

Once you have the right shoes, follow this simple plan:

  • Week 1–2: Walk 4 minutes, run 1 minute, repeat 6 times.
  • Week 3–4: Walk 3 minutes, run 2 minutes, repeat 6 times.
  • Week 5–6: Walk 2 minutes, run 3 minutes, repeat 6 times.
  • Week 7–8: Walk 1 minute, run 4 minutes, repeat 6 times.

By the end of 8 weeks, you'll be running 30 minutes straight without stopping. This is the safest way to build endurance without injury.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need expensive running shoes as a beginner?

No. A good ₹1,500–₹3,000 running shoe is more than enough for your first year of running. Expensive shoes are for experienced runners who've built up mileage.

Can I run in normal sneakers?

You can, but you shouldn't. Regular sneakers don't have the right cushioning for running, which can lead to knee pain and shin splints. Get a proper running shoe.

How often should I replace my running shoes?

Every 500–700 km. For a beginner running 20 km a week, that's roughly 8–12 months. After that, the cushioning flattens and the shoe loses its protective properties.
