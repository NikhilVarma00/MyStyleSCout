What to Look For in Sneakers Under ₹800

Operating within an ultra-tight budget requires prioritizing key functional attributes:

  • Mesh Uppers: Prioritize thermal airflow over synthetic leather; mesh dries faster and prevents moisture retention.
  • EVA Midsoles: Seek out EVA foam outsoles to secure the optimal balance of cushion and wear-resistance at this tier.
  • Sizing Adjustments: Domestic value brands frequently feature snug fits; consider sizing up by 0.5 or 1 size if you have wider feet.
  • Realistic Expectations: A sub-₹600 shoe is designed for high-value daily service, yielding roughly 6 to 12 months of active wear before natural flattening.
