What to Look For in a Sub-₹1500 Sneaker

At this price, you can't expect premium materials. But you should expect:

  • EVA or rubber sole — not the cheap plastic soles used in ₹300 shoes
  • Stitched upper — glued-only uppers fall apart in monsoon
  • Correct size — always size up by 0.5 if buying ASIAN or Sparx
  • Genuine discount — check the price history before buying

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the best sneaker brand under ₹1500 in India?

Puma is the best brand under ₹1500, especially when the Flyer Flex is on sale. Sparx and Campus offer the best value at the very bottom of the price range (under ₹800). ASIAN is a close third if you want more design variety.

Are ₹1500 sneakers good enough for daily use?

Yes. A well-chosen ₹1500 sneaker will easily last 1–1.5 years of daily college or office wear. Just avoid the cheapest models if you walk more than 5km a day.
