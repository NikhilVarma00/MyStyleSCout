What to Look For in Sneakers Under ₹1500

Operating within a budget tier requires prioritizing key performance factors:

  • Thermal Ventilation: Prioritize engineered mesh uppers to keep feet cool in humid Indian climates.
  • Outsole Material: High-density EVA and rubber soles offer superior longevity compared to cheap polyurethane alternatives.
  • Sizing Awareness: Domestic labels like Sparx and ASIAN often run snug; consult size charts before checkout.
  • Intended Purpose: Avoid using casual walking shoes for long-distance running; match footwear directly to your activity.
