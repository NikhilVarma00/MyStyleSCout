How StyleScout Works

Finding the right sneakers at the right price shouldn't mean checking multiple shopping apps one by one. StyleScout makes it easier to discover sneakers, compare prices and explore shopping options across Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra.

In a nutshell: StyleScout brings sneaker listings and available retailer prices together in one place, helping you compare your options and decide where to shop.

Why We Built StyleScout

The same pair of sneakers can be listed at different prices across online shopping platforms. Discounts, offers, sizes and availability can also vary between retailers.

Comparing all these options manually can take time. We created StyleScout to make that process simpler by bringing sneaker information and retailer links together in one convenient place.

How StyleScout Works

Step 1 — Discover Sneakers

Explore our sneaker collection and discover options from popular brands across different styles, categories and price ranges.

Step 2 — Explore Retailer Prices

Check the available listings for a sneaker across supported shopping platforms, including Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra, where available.

Step 3 — Compare Your Options

View the listed prices side by side and identify the lowest displayed price among the available listings. Compare product variants and seller details before making your decision.

Step 4 — Visit the Retailer

When you choose a shopping option, click its shopping link to visit the retailer's website. You can review the final product details and complete your purchase directly with that retailer.

What You Can Find on StyleScout

  • Sneaker listings: Discover sneakers from different brands and categories.
  • Price comparisons: Compare displayed prices across supported retailers.
  • Lowest-price indicators: Identify the lowest listed price among the available options shown on a product page.
  • Price history: Explore historical price information where available.
  • Product details: Review available specifications, descriptions and images.
  • Related products: Discover other sneakers that may match your interests.

How We Make Money

StyleScout is free to browse. Some of the shopping links on our website are affiliate links.

This means we may earn a commission when you click certain retailer links and complete qualifying purchases. This may include links to Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra, subject to our participation in their respective affiliate programmes.

Affiliate commissions do not add a separate affiliate charge to your purchase. The retailer determines the final price you pay.

You can read more on our Affiliate Disclosure page.

What StyleScout Does Not Do

  • We do not directly sell the sneakers listed on our website.
  • We do not process retailer payments or handle deliveries.
  • We do not manage retailer returns or refunds.
  • We do not control retailer prices or stock availability.
  • We do not guarantee that a displayed offer will remain available.

Why Prices May Differ

Retailer prices and offers can change. A product's price may also vary depending on its size, colour, seller, location, delivery charges or promotional offers.

There may be a delay between a retailer changing a price and the updated information appearing on StyleScout. Therefore, a displayed price should be treated as a reference rather than a guaranteed checkout price.

Always verify the final price, product variant, seller and availability on the retailer's website before completing your purchase.
