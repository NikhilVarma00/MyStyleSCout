How to Save on Sneakers in India

1. Compare Prices Across All Stores

The same shoe can cost wildly different amounts on Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra. Don't buy from the first store you find. Use StyleScout to compare prices in seconds.

2. Watch for Sales (And Buy During Them)

The biggest discounts happen during major sales like:

  • Amazon Great Indian Festival
  • Flipkart Big Billion Days
  • Myntra End of Reason Sale (EORS)

3. Check Price History

Not every "discount" is real. Some platforms inflate the MRP to make the discount look bigger. Our product pages show the price history so you can tell if a discount is genuine.

4. Don't Ignore Bank Offers

Most platforms offer additional 10% discounts with specific banks. Combining a sale price with a bank offer is where the real savings happen.
