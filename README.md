# MyStyleScout — Find. Compare. Save.

**Live Website:** https://www.mystylescout.in

MyStyleScout is an India-focused sneaker discovery and price comparison website. It helps users compare the same sneaker across online retailers such as Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra, making it easier to explore available prices and find deals.

## Features

- **Product Discovery:** Browse sneakers from multiple online retailers.
- **Price Comparison:** Compare available prices for the same sneaker across stores.
- **Search:** Find products using search.
- **Filters:** Filter by brand, store, price and discount.
- **Sorting:** Sort products by best deal and other available options.
- **Quick View:** Preview product information without opening the full detail page.
- **Product Details:** Short titles on product cards and full product titles on detail pages.
- **Wishlist:** Save products for later.

## Price Information

- Product and price information is maintained in a static JSON catalog.
- Prices are updated manually and are not fetched through live retailer APIs.
- The displayed prices may not reflect the latest retailer prices.
- Price comparisons are based on the information currently available in the catalog.
- Always verify the final price and availability on the retailer's website before purchasing.

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS and Vanilla JavaScript
- **JavaScript:** `script.js`
- **Product Data:** `product.json`
- **Rendering:** Products are rendered dynamically using JavaScript.
- **Search, Filters and Sorting:** Handled on the client side.

## How It Works

1. Product information and retailer prices are stored in `product.json`.
2. `script.js` reads the catalog and renders product cards and details.
3. Users can search, filter and sort the available products directly in their browser.
4. Product links take users to the respective retailer websites.
