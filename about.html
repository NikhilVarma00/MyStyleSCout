About StyleScout

Better sneaker choices start with better comparisons. StyleScout helps shoppers in India explore sneaker prices across popular shopping platforms, so they can make more informed buying decisions without checking every store separately.

Our idea is simple: make comparing sneaker prices easier, make shopping decisions clearer, and help you find options that fit your budget and style.

Why We Built StyleScout

Finding the right pair of sneakers is about more than just choosing a design. Prices can differ between shopping platforms, offers change, and finding the best available deal can take time.

We created StyleScout to make that process simpler. Instead of checking multiple shopping websites one by one, you can use StyleScout to explore sneaker options and compare available prices in one place.

What You Can Do on StyleScout

Our platform is designed to help you:

  • Explore sneakers from different brands and styles.
  • Compare listed prices across Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra.
  • Find the retailer offering the lowest listed price for a product.
  • Explore available product details and price-history information.
  • Visit retailer websites to check the latest offers and purchase options.

Our goal is to make the comparison process convenient. Product prices, stock, sizes and offers may change, so always confirm the final details on the retailer's website before placing an order.

How StyleScout Works

StyleScout brings together sneaker listings and available pricing information from supported shopping platforms. When you find a product you're interested in, you can compare the displayed retailer options and follow the link to the relevant shopping website.

Where price-history information is available, it can help you understand how a product's listed price has changed over time. Historical prices should be treated as a guide, not a guarantee of future prices or discounts.

How We Make Money

StyleScout is free to browse. Some links on our website are affiliate links. If you follow one of these links and make a qualifying purchase, we may earn a commission from the retailer at no additional cost to you.

Affiliate relationships help support the operation and development of StyleScout. They do not change the price you pay to the retailer.

We aim to present product and price information clearly. Retailer prices, availability and other commercial terms should always be verified on the retailer's website.

For more details, read our Affiliate Disclosure.

Our Approach

We believe shopping should be informed, straightforward and transparent. We're building StyleScout around that idea, with a focus on useful comparisons and a simple experience for sneaker shoppers.
